# Sales & Accounts guide — demand side

From cold prospect to signed contract to healthy long-term account, every
touch lives on a timeline the whole commercial team can see.

## Prospecting (top of funnel)

- **Prospects** are pre-pipeline: company, contact, source, fit score 1–5.
- **Cadences** automate the touch sequence: define steps once (kind + note +
  day offset); starting a cadence on a prospect creates today's task, and
  completing each task schedules the next automatically. One active cadence
  per prospect.
- **Today view** is your work queue — cadence touches and check-ins due
  today. Work it top to bottom; completions log to the prospect's timeline.
- **Do-not-contact is a kill switch**: it stops the active cadence, cancels
  open tasks, and blocks conversion — permanently. Honor it.
- **Convert** promotes a responding prospect to a lead, carrying the history.

## Pipeline (Sales)

Stages: `lead → qualified → proposal sent → negotiation → won/lost`, with
probabilities driving the weighted pipeline number. Drag a card one column
forward (the valid target lights up) or use the lead workspace — either way
it's the same server-validated action. Skipping stages is refused; early
**win** from qualified is allowed (deals sometimes just close).

- **Losing requires a comment** — loss reasons are the cheapest market
  research you'll ever get.
- Log calls/notes/emails on the lead timeline; the composer sends from your
  address or a department identity your role allows.

## Quotes

Versioned, immutable-per-version: create → add lines (role, hours, rate) →
**send** → client responds. Changes after sending? **Revise** — v2 copies the
lines, v1 becomes `superseded`. Accept/reject records the outcome on the
version that was actually on the table.

## Winning — the handoff wizard

**Win** does the delivery setup in one transaction: creates (or links) the
client, the contract (tied to the accepted quote), the project, and — if you
sold specific roles — the staffing request for resourcing. Finance is
notified to complete billing setup (rates, terms). Nothing to remember,
nothing to forget.

## Contracts & renewals

Contracts track start/end and the commercial basis. The renewal watchdog
notifies sales+owner at **60 and 30 days** before end, and expires ended
contracts automatically. Renewal = a new contract, usually via a fresh quote.

## Accounts (after the win)

**Account 360** per client: team on the ground, hours this month, open and
overdue AR, next renewal, open opportunities, escalations, and the health
score.

- **Health score** is explainable — every penalty is named (overdue AR,
  falling hours, negative feedback, open escalation). Red accounts notify
  the account owner; don't wait for the client to call.
- **Opportunities** track upsell (idea → proposed → won/lost) and feed the
  two-sided pipeline the owner sees.
- **Escalations**: open one when a client relationship needs care — it
  automatically holds the overdue-tone dunning mail while you fix things
  (courtesy reminders still go). Resolving it resumes collections. Finance
  and owner are notified on open.
- **Check-ins**: recurring per-account touch tasks land in your Today view.

## Client portal administration

Use **Clients -> <client> -> Client portal** for external client contacts,
shared documents and client approval requests. Client contacts are not
internal users and must not be invited through **Admin -> People**.

Common account-owner examples:

| Need | Do |
|---|---|
| Client sponsor should receive approval requests | Add them as a portal user |
| Client needs SOW/status pack/invoice support | Add a client-visible document record |
| Client must approve monthly timesheets | Create an approval request linked to the project |
| Client asks for delivery context | Use account timeline, project productivity items and approved timesheet summaries |

See the [Client portal guide](client-portal.md) for the full workflow and
current access boundary.
