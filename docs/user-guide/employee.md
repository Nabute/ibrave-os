# Employee guide — time, payouts, profile

Your hours are the raw material of the whole system: they become client
invoices, your payout, and the company's margin. Logging them accurately and
on time is the one thing the system needs from you.

## The weekly timesheet (My Timesheet)

Rows are your project/task assignments, columns are Mon–Sun. Click a cell,
type hours, press Enter — the cell flashes when saved.

- Hours are in **0.25 steps** (15 minutes): `6.5`, `7.25`. `1.13` is rejected.
- Max 24h per day across all rows; zero-hour entries aren't stored.
- **Billable vs internal**: billable is the default on client projects;
  internal work (meetings, training) is tracked but never invoiced.
- Missing a project? You need an assignment — ask your PM. The system refuses
  time on projects you're not assigned to on that date.
- **Copy last week** clones last week's grid as drafts — edit and go.

### Submit, and what happens next

**Submit week** sends every draft to your project's PM (they're notified).

| State | Cell marker | Can you edit? |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | plain | Yes — freely |
| Submitted | blue rail | No — locked until approved or rejected |
| Approved | green rail | Never — it's now billing material |

- **Rejected?** The entry returns to draft with the PM's comment (you're
  notified). Fix and resubmit.
- **Approved but wrong?** Approved entries are immutable. Add an **adjustment
  entry**: a new entry (can be negative) referencing the original — the
  correction is visible instead of history being rewritten.
- Monday mornings you'll be reminded about any unsubmitted previous week.

## Time off

Record vacation/sick days under Staffing → Time off (you can only record your
own). It shows on the bench so resourcing doesn't plan you into work while
you're away.

## Payouts

Payouts → your statements only. Finance generates them from your **approved**
hours × your cost rate; you're notified when a statement is confirmed and when
it's paid. The hours on your statement always reconcile to the entries you
submitted — if something looks off, the workflow history on the statement
shows exactly which entries it covers.

## If you interview candidates

When a recruiter schedules you as an interviewer, that candidate becomes
visible to you (and only that candidate). Fill your scorecard and
recommendation after the round — hiring is blocked until at least one
scorecard is submitted.
