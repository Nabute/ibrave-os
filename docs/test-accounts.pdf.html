Talent pipeline

A candidate waits at every stage: sourced, screening, interview, assessment, offer, hired, rejected with a reason, and parked in the talent pool.

The hiring gate. The offer-stage candidate has a submitted scorecard, so hiring succeeds. The interview-stage candidate has an unsubmitted round, so hiring must stay blocked until someone completes it.

Requisitions: one open with two seats, one already filled. The hired candidate carries a full onboarding checklist.

12

Suggested test pass

  1. Sign in as each role account and confirm the sidebar matches the role matrix exactly, no more and no less.
  2. Confirm the deactivated account is refused, and that the two-factor account is forced into enrollment before it can reach the workspace.
  3. As the employee account: log time, submit the week, then confirm the entries lock and can no longer be edited.
  4. As the project manager: approve part of that week, reject one entry with a comment, then confirm the employee's copy returns to draft carrying the reason.
  5. As finance: generate a draft from approved work, issue it and check the numbering format, try to edit it (must fail), record a partial payment, then raise a credit note.
  6. As sales: drag a lead one stage forward on the board, then run the win handoff and confirm a client, contract, project and staffing request all appear.
  7. As the recruiter: try to hire the interview-stage candidate (blocked for want of a scorecard), then hire the offer-stage one and confirm the onboarding checklist appears.
  8. Across every list: use the search box, the filter dropdowns and the bulk selection bar, and confirm a filtered-out row can never be caught by a bulk action.
